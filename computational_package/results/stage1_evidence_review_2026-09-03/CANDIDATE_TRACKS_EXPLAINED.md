# Why the candidate sheets contain different proteins

There are two separate candidate-selection tracks. They should not be treated as one continuously updated ranking table.

## Track A: V3 structural shortlist

Source: the V3 `candidate_selection_table.csv`, now labeled `01_v3_internal_structural_shortlist_not_final_stage1.csv`.

This rule selected V3 rows with an eligible evidence tier, a robust declared register, and complete structural-surface information. It produced four rows:

- EBNA-LP–CRYAB on DRB1*03:01
- BLLF1_gp350–CLDN11 on DRB1*13:03
- BALF4_gB–MAG on DRB1*15:01
- BARF1–MAG in the combined DRB1*15:01 universe

This is a V3 structural shortlist. It is not the later eight-candidate Stage-1 evidence review.

## Track B: later eight-candidate evidence review

Source: `stage1_assay_recommendations.csv` and `candidate_evidence_matrix.csv`.

This additive dossier audited eight preselected sequence-supported candidates using binding, register, provenance, immunopeptidome, conservation, and rarity evidence. It explicitly did not rerank the full discovery universe.

Its outcome was:

- Medium priority: BALF5–TALDO1 on DRB1*13:03
- Medium priority: BALF5–TALDO1 on DRB1*15:01
- Hold: the other six candidates
- High priority: none

The recommended next experiment for both BALF5–TALDO1 pairs is exact-HLA peptide binding plus nested-peptide register mapping.

## Correct interpretation for the meeting

BALF5–TALDO1 should be described as the best Stage-1 experimental leads among the eight candidates that received the later evidence audit. They are not the top members of the separate four-row V3 structural shortlist, and they are not proven molecular mimics.

The project currently lacks one consolidated final ranking that applies every gate to every candidate in the full V3 universe. That is why the two sheets should be presented as complementary tracks rather than as contradictory versions of one final list.
