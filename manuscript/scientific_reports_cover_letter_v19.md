Dear Editors,

Please consider our Article, “Register-aware computation prioritizes EBV–self peptide pairs for experimental testing across HLA class II contexts,” for publication in *Scientific Reports*.

This study presents a reproducible, register-aware computational workflow for prioritizing Epstein–Barr virus (EBV) and human self-peptide pairs for experimental investigation in HLA class II contexts. Across 6,400 allele-specific comparisons and a separate enriched evaluation panel, the study identifies two BALF5–TALDO1 pairs for subsequent peptide–HLA binding and register-mapping experiments. It also evaluates how register choice and modeling inputs affect interpretation. The manuscript deliberately distinguishes computational prioritization from evidence of natural presentation, T-cell cross-reactivity, molecular mimicry, or multiple-sclerosis causality.

The work is appropriate for *Scientific Reports* because it provides a methodologically transparent computational analysis with public code, input manifests, derived data, and supplementary materials. The manuscript reports its limitations directly, preserves frozen outputs and reproducibility documentation, and identifies concrete experiments needed to test the proposed candidates.
